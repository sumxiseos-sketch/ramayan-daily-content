Ramayan Daily — Privacy Policy

Published by Shiny Sphere · Effective 21 November 2026

In short: Ramayan Daily has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your reading progress, favourites and private journal stay on your device. We never receive them.

1. Who we are

Ramayan Daily is a daily reading app of passages from the Ramayana, published by Shiny Sphere in Fiji. Questions about this policy: sumxiseos@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

  • Your reading progress: the days you marked complete and your streaks.
  • Your favourite passages and recently viewed passages.
  • Your private reflection journal entries.
  • Your settings, such as language, theme and text size, and your daily reminder choice and time.

This information is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone. We do not have access to it.

3. What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or microphone data. It does not use an advertising ID, an analytics service or a crash-reporting service.

4. Content updates over the internet

To bring you new passages, the app downloads a public content file hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. This is an ordinary web request over HTTPS. It does not send your journal, favourites, progress or any other personal information. As with any website, GitHub’s servers can see technical details such as your IP address and the time of the request, which GitHub handles under its own privacy statement. We do not operate that server and do not receive those logs. If you are offline, the app keeps working with the passages already on your device.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: used only for the content updates described above.
  • Notifications: requested only when you turn on a daily reminder. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your phone’s settings.

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. When you share a wisdom card, the app uses Android’s share sheet. The card contains the passage, its source reference and the app name, and never your journal.

6. Your journal and backups

Journal entries are private and are never transmitted by the app. You can edit or delete any entry at any time. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app permanently removes your entries, and we cannot recover them because we never receive a copy. If Android’s device backup is turned on, your phone may include the app’s data in your own Google account backup. That is controlled by your device and Google settings, not by us.

7. Children

Ramayan Daily is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Third parties

We do not sell or share personal information, and the app contains no advertising or analytics software. If you installed the app from Google Play, Google’s own privacy policy applies to your use of Google Play.

9. Security

Downloads use HTTPS. App data is stored in private app storage that other apps cannot normally read. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please avoid writing anything in your journal that you would not want exposed if your phone were lost without a screen lock.

10. Your choices

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control everything on your device: edit or delete journal entries, clear app data, or uninstall the app. You can contact us at any time with questions.

11. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
