Ramayan Daily Premium - Privacy Policy

Published by Shiny Sphere. Effective 27 November 2026.

Ramayan Daily Premium ("the app") is an app for exploring the Ramayana. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

In short

The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not need an internet connection to work.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not use the internet. The app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send your information anywhere.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It does not show advertising and does not include advertising or tracking software.

Information on your device

The scripture passages, characters and maps are bundled inside the app. If the app saves anything about how you use it, such as settings or your place in the text, that information stays on your device only and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Google Play

You get the app through Google Play. Google handles your purchase and payment details under Google's own privacy policy, and we do not receive your card or payment information. Google Play may also give us aggregated, anonymous reports, such as crash counts, from users who have chosen to share diagnostics with Google.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
